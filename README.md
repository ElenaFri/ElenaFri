## 🛠 What I Enjoy Working On

**Linux, from the inside.** I enjoy the parts of a system most people never look at. Permissions, packaging, the way a distribution fits together, you name it. Building Gentoo from source taught me how the pieces connect; Debian and the BSDs taught me what a stable system looks like. I like understanding why a machine behaves the way it does - and making it work too, of course.

**Infrastructure that runs itself.** My favourite kind of sysadmin work is the kind you only have to do once: automating deployments with Puppet or Ansible, and Bash for scripting, containerising services with Docker, keeping secrets in Vault, serving everything through a well-tuned Nginx or Caddy, and wiring up CI/CD on GitHub and GitLab.

**Software that respects its users.** On the development side, I'm drawn to systems programming in C and C++, and to native Linux applications that keep your data on your own machine: no server, no account, properly packaged as `.deb` and AppImage, etc. I'm also at ease on the web back-end (PHP, TypeScript, PostgreSQL).

**Where words meet code.** I care about clear documentation, localisation and text tooling.
